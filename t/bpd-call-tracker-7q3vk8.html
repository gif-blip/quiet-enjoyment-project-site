BPD call tracker

Fill this in after you call Boulder Police about a party. It takes three minutes. Nothing you type is stored on this website: when you tap Send, your email app opens with the answers filled in, addressed to the Quiet Enjoyment Project. Attach your recording to that email before you send it.

Colorado law allows you to record your own phone calls (C.R.S. 18-9-303); a recording of your call with dispatch or an officer is the best record there is. The Quiet Enjoyment Project is not a law firm, and this page is not legal advice. QEP uses what you report by address and in aggregate and does not publish callers' names.
