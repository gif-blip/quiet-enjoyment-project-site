# Boulder Police noise charge file, October 21, 2022 – October 20, 2025
Produced to The Quiet Enjoyment Project by the Boulder Police Department Records unit under the Colorado Criminal Justice Records Act (Request ID 1392348766), delivered as `Noise Charges, 2022-2025.xlsx`; republished here unchanged except for a CSV copy. The file spans January 4, 2022 to October 31, 2025 (615 rows); the reports count only the three-year window October 21, 2022 – October 20, 2025 that BPD's calls-for-service extract covers.

Columns: ReportNumber · Date · Section (B.R.C. or C.R.S. section charged) · Description · Address · Person_Charged. **Person_Charged = "Yes" means a citation was issued; blank means a report was taken and no one was charged** (BPD Records Manager, written clarification, February 18, 2026). 615 rows; 528 with a person charged.

No names or personal identifiers are in the file. Addresses are the incident addresses as BPD recorded them.

How the reports use it: party-noise citations (sections 5-9-5 quiet enjoyment, 5-9-6 unreasonable noise, 5-3-x nuisance party) = 67 charged of 114 rows in the window; barking-dog citations (6-1-19) = 312 charged of 331 rows in the window. The complaint denominators (8,192 noise complaints; 13,065 animal calls) come from the City of Boulder's public calls-for-service open-data feed for the same window.
